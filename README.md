# DeFi Opportunity Scout
### Telegraph Protocol Hackathon Season II

## 1. Project Overview

**DeFi Opportunity Scout** is a DeFi research platform being developed for the **Telegraph Protocol Hackathon Season II**.

The project helps users discover, compare, and evaluate DeFi yield opportunities using their preferences, available market data, and additional intelligence purchased through Telegraph.

Instead of focusing only on advertised APY, the platform considers factors such as liquidity, fees, withdrawal conditions, protocol risks, and the quality and freshness of available evidence.

**Core idea:** Help users make better-informed DeFi decisions by bringing opportunity discovery and intelligence-backed research into one place.

## 2. The Problem

DeFi users often struggle to compare yield opportunities because information is scattered across protocols, analytics platforms, and intelligence providers.

A high advertised APY does not necessarily mean an opportunity is suitable. Users also need to understand liquidity, fees, withdrawal restrictions, potential risks, and whether the information is reliable and up to date.

Researching these factors manually takes time and can leave important questions unanswered.

## 3. The Solution

DeFi Opportunity Scout acts as a research assistant for DeFi opportunities.

Users provide their preferences, explore available opportunities, and request additional intelligence when necessary. The platform uses Telegraph to purchase relevant intelligence from available providers and incorporates the returned information into its research reports.

Users can then compare opportunities, review supporting evidence, understand uncertainties, and decide what to do themselves.

The platform provides research and recommendations. It does not automatically invest or move users' funds.

## 4. Core Features

### Opportunity Discovery
Users explore DeFi yield opportunities and filter or sort them using available information, including network, yield, liquidity, and other relevant metrics.

### Personalised Preferences
Users provide their preferred networks, budget range, and risk preference to help organise relevant opportunities.

### Intelligence-Backed Research
Users request additional intelligence through Telegraph when suitable providers and integrations are available. The platform incorporates the returned information into its research process.

### Research Reports
Reports present relevant findings, supporting evidence, source information, data freshness, potential risks, missing information, and limitations.

### Opportunity Comparison
Users compare opportunities using multiple factors rather than relying exclusively on headline APY.

### Watchlist and Research History
Users save opportunities and revisit previous research requests and reports.

## 5. Telegraph Integration

Telegraph is central to the project's technical purpose.

The platform is designed to demonstrate a meaningful machine-to-machine purchase of intelligence through Telegraph, followed by the use of that intelligence in a practical DeFi research task.

The intended workflow is:

1. Identify an opportunity that needs further investigation.
2. Request relevant intelligence through Telegraph.
3. Purchase the intelligence from an available provider.
4. Receive and record the actual response and available purchase evidence.
5. Incorporate the findings into the research report.

Purchase costs, provider identities, receipts, and request statuses must reflect actual integration responses. Unavailable or simulated functionality must be clearly identified.

## 6. Hackathon Scope

The initial version focuses on opportunity discovery, intelligence-backed research, comparison, and evidence-based recommendations.

Automated portfolio allocation, trading, deposits, withdrawals, and custody of user funds are outside the initial scope.

The priority is to demonstrate a coherent product in which Telegraph's purchasing capabilities directly support a useful research workflow.

## 7. Data Integrity

The platform must not fabricate market data, research findings, provider responses, or Telegraph receipts.

It should distinguish verified information from provider claims and derived analysis, identify missing or outdated evidence, and communicate uncertainty clearly.

Research conclusions are not guarantees of safety, suitability, or future returns.

## 8. Success Criteria

The project aims to demonstrate that users can discover DeFi opportunities, request additional intelligence through Telegraph, review evidence-backed research, and compare alternatives in one experience.

The key technical demonstration is a genuine intelligence purchase through Telegraph whose returned information contributes to the research outcome.

## Summary

**DeFi Opportunity Scout turns Telegraph's machine-to-machine purchasing capabilities into a practical DeFi research experience.**

By combining opportunity discovery, purchased intelligence, and evidence-based comparison, the project aims to help users investigate DeFi opportunities more efficiently and make better-informed decisions.
